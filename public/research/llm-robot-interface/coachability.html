Can you steer a robot with a prompt reward function?

Follow-up study on coachability in agentic robotics (coming soon).

Trevor Kwan In progress

In the SO-101 interface study, both setups received an explicit utility function in the prompt alongside a live dollar and clock meter: R = 100S − 20(p − S)² − 4C − 2T.

This upcoming follow-up sweeps those weights directly on physical hardware to measure how faithfully frontier models actually change their speed, verification habits, and precision tradeoffs when you change the objective in text, without retraining or collecting new demonstrations.
